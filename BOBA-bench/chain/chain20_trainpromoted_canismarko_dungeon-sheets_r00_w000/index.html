Given a human-friendly name, findattr locates the corresponding attribute on obj and returns its value:

Whenever a weapon is wielded, its attack_bonus and bonus_damage must be computed on the basis of the character's ability scores and proficiency:

  • From an ability score, the relevant ability modifier is derived with the standard D&D formula: modifier = (score - 10) // 2.
  • With a finesse weapon, the ability that gets used is whichever of Strength or Dexterity yields the higher modifier.
  • attack_bonus = the ability modifier that was chosen, plus the character's proficiency bonus if the character is proficient with that weapon; should the character lack proficiency, the proficiency bonus is not added.
  • bonus_damage = the ability modifier that was chosen (no proficiency bonus is included).

What decides proficiency is whether the weapon's class shows up in the character's weapon_proficienies list (an attribute that holds a list of weapon classes).

One concrete illustration of the arithmetic that is required:

  • self.proficiencies_extra: a tuple/list of strings holding additional proficiency phrases (it may be empty/absent by default).

The combined text is produced as follows: join all the phrases (the base ones first, the extras after them) using ', ', capitalize the very first letter of the string that results, and append a period . at the end.

Examples:

  • _proficiencies_text = ('hello', 'world') and no extras set → 'Hello, world.'
  • proficiencies_extra = ("it's", "me") set on top of that → "Hello, world, it's, me."

Note: capitalization touches only the first character of the entire combined string; the phrases after it keep the casing they originally had.
