Character.armor_class

Armor class is calculated as:

  1. the worn body armor's base_armor_class, or 10 when no body armor is worn;
  2. plus the character's Dexterity modifier;
  3. plus the equipped shield's armor-class bonus, or 0 when no shield is equipped.

The body armor's base armor class replaces the unarmored base of 10; it is not added on top of 10. The standard Shield bonus is 2.

The Dexterity modifier is the standard value floor((dexterity - 10) / 2), equivalently (dexterity - 10) // 2 for integer scores. In particular, Dexterity 16 has a modifier of +3.

If the equipped body armor has a non-None dexterity_mod_max, its Dexterity contribution is min(actual_dexterity_modifier, dexterity_mod_max). If the value is None, the full modifier is used.

For a character with Dexterity 16:

  • LeatherArmor has base armor class 11 and no cap, so armor_class == 14.
  • If that armor instance's dexterity_mod_max is changed to 1, armor_class == 12.
  • With no body armor and a standard Shield, armor_class == 10 + 3 + 2 == 15.

Module: dungeonsheets.character
Class: Druid
Related modules: dungeonsheets.monsters and dungeonsheets.exceptions

A Druid supports level=<int>, wild_shapes=[...], and the optional public attribute circle=<string> during construction. Omitting circle, or using a circle other than 'moon', uses the standard-Druid limits described below.

Wild-shape assignment and resolution
  • Druid.wild_shapes may be assigned a collection containing monster names, existing dungeonsheets.monsters.Monster instances, or a mixture of the two.
  • Names resolve without regard to capitalization and tolerate normal spacing between words, such as 'Wolf', 'giant eagle', and 'ankylosaurus'.
  • A resolved name is instantiated as its corresponding monster class.
  • An existing Monster instance is accepted directly and retained as that instance; it is not re-resolved or replaced.
  • If any name cannot be resolved to a known monster, assignment raises dungeonsheets.exceptions.MonsterError.
  • A known but currently ineligible monster is not an unknown-name error. It remains a resolved candidate but is omitted from the filtered wild_shapes result.
  • character.all_wild_shapes exposes the resolved candidates regardless of current eligibility. character.wild_shapes returns a list containing only candidates for which character.can_assume_shape(...) is true at the character's current level and circle.

In particular, 'giant eagle' must be a recognized name supplied by the monsters module. For a level-2 standard Druid, assigning ['Wolf', 'giant eagle'] succeeds and the filtered wild_shapes list contains only the Wolf.

Challenge-rating limits

A Druid below level 2 cannot assume any wild shape, regardless of circle or monster attributes.

For a standard Druid at level 2 or above, the maximum challenge rating is:

Druid level Maximum CR
2–3 1/4
4–7 1/2
8+ 1

For a Circle of the Moon Druid (circle='moon'), the maximum challenge rating is:

Druid level Maximum CR
2–5 1
6+ level // 3

Thus a level-2 standard Druid can include a CR-1/4 Wolf; a level-2 Moon Druid can include a CR-1 Ape; and a level-9 Moon Druid can include a CR-3 Ankylosaurus because 9 // 3 == 3.

Beast and movement restrictions

Challenge rating is not the only eligibility condition. All of the following must hold:

  • The monster is a beast. A monster whose type or description does not identify it as a beast is rejected.
  • At levels below 4, a monster with a positive swim_speed is rejected.
  • At levels 4 and above, positive swim speed is allowed.
  • At levels below 8, a monster with a positive fly_speed is rejected.
  • At levels 8 and above, positive fly speed is allowed.
  • Zero swim or fly speed does not trigger the corresponding restriction.

These movement limits apply to both standard and Moon Druids in addition to their respective challenge-rating limits.

  • A subclass of monsters.Monster with description = 'beast' and the base low challenge rating and zero movement speeds is accepted by a level-2 standard Druid but rejected by a level-1 Druid.

The dungeonsheets.monsters module must expose at least the known classes needed by these APIs: Monster, Wolf, GiantEagle, Ape, and Ankylosaurus.

  • Wolf is a beast with CR 1/4.
