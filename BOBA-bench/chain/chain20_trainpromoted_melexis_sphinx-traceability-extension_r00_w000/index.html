  • Existing relation-query options that distinguish explicit and implicit targets must continue to reflect the relation provenance after purging.

Compatibility and Scope

  • Add purge_item without removing or renaming any existing public method. Existing APIs such as item addition, relation addition, item-level relation queries, document-level purging, and any existing item-removal operation must remain available and retain their established behavior.
  • Apart from making TraceabilityException a ValueError subclass as required above, do not redesign unrelated APIs.
  • No particular internal storage strategy for removed items is required; only the externally observable behavior and state isolation described here are required.
