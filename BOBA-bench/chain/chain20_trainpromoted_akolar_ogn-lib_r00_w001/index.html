  • 'stealth': the value taken from the parsed ID; in the example, False.
  • 'uid': the value taken from the parsed ID; in the example, '1C4007220E'.
  • 'turn_rate': the turn rate, parsed as a float from the rot token; for +0.0rot, 0.0.
