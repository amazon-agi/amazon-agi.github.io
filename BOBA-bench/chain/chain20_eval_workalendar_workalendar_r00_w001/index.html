Objective

Implement full United Kingdom holiday functionality within the workalendar.europe module. The public class to be used is the already-existing UnitedKingdomCalendar. This class is required to deliver the Boxing Day and observed-holiday behavior laid out below.

  • Module: workalendar.europe

holidays_set(year) returns a set of datetime.date objects corresponding to the United Kingdom holidays for the requested calendar year. The package's calendar base infrastructure may continue to provide this method, but UnitedKingdomCalendar must supply the holiday behavior necessary to generate the dates listed below.

Required Holiday Behavior

Fixed and computed holidays

For any given year, the returned set must contain the following nominal holiday dates:

  • New Year's Day: January 1.
  • Spring Bank Holiday: the last Monday of May.
  • Late Summer Bank Holiday: the last Monday of August.
  • Christmas Day: December 25.
  • Boxing Day: December 26.

Additional movable holidays, such as Easter-related holidays the calendar already supports, may be present as well, but this task imposes no further restrictions on their dates. The dates listed here are requirements for inclusion, not a complete enumeration of the set.

Weekend observation and cascading substitutions

If one of the applicable holidays occurs on a Saturday or Sunday, an observed holiday must be added on the next available working day. The original weekend date must also remain in the set; observation appends a date and does not replace the nominal holiday date.

When consecutive holidays or their substitutions would collide, observed dates must cascade. Every affected holiday must be given its own distinct subsequent working day, meaning two shifted holidays cannot land on the same date. This rule also covers the situation where the first weekday after a weekend holiday is already taken by the next holiday in a consecutive holiday period.

Required Examples

  • August 26, 2013 — Late Summer Bank Holiday.

  • January 1, 2012 — the nominal New Year's Day date.
