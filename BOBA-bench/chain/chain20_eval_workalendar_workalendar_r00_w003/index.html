Background

ChileCalendar is still available from workalendar.america:

The existing method of ChileCalendar:

holidays_set(year)

must keep returning a set of datetime.date objects that represent the calendar's observed public holidays for year. None of the public API signatures change.

Behavior requirements

Derive the Reformation Day observance from the weekday of October 31 in the requested year:

Weekday of October 31 Reformation Day observance
Monday October 31
Tuesday The preceding Friday, October 27
Wednesday The following Friday, November 2
Thursday October 31
Friday October 31
Saturday October 31
Sunday October 31

Consequently, Reformation Day is shifted away from October 31 only when October 31 is a Tuesday or a Wednesday:

  • When October 31 is a Tuesday, holidays_set(year) must include October 27 and must not include October 31 as the Reformation Day observance.
  • When October 31 is a Wednesday, holidays_set(year) must include November 2 and must not include October 31 as the Reformation Day observance.
  • When October 31 falls on any other weekday, holidays_set(year) must keep October 31 as the Reformation Day observance; that observance must not be shifted to another Friday.
