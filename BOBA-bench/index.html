Finding 4 of 4

One bit from outside recovers much of the loss

Verification and repair

What this means

Trust, but verify

For system builders

Let the model hear the verdict: one bit from whoever can run the result recovers a third to a half of the misses.

For model builders

An agent that can be left alone must tell its wrong implementations from its correct ones; today the best barely beats chance.

For benchmark builders

Grade in chains, with a hidden contract the agent cannot rewrite, and report completion with and without a supervisor.

For users

Check before you build on it, and say what you saw: naming what failed is worth about three rounds of insisting.
